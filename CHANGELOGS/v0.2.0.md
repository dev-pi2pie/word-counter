## What's Changed

- Word counts keep contractions such as `Don't` and `they’re` and decimals such as `3.14` and `١٫٢` intact when the locale segmenter returns them as a single word. The same rule keeps the underscore in `foo_bar` inside the word.
- Standalone punctuation such as `___` and `٫` counts as zero words. With `--non-words`, `___` is one punctuation entry. In character mode with non-words enabled, its three underscores count as three non-word graphemes.
