## What's Changed

- Word counts keep contractions such as `Don't` and `they’re` and decimals such as `3.14` and `١٫٢` intact when the locale segmenter returns them as a single word. The same rule keeps the underscore in `foo_bar` inside the word.
- Standalone punctuation such as `___` and `٫` counts as zero words. With `--non-words`, `___` is one punctuation entry. In character mode with non-words enabled, its three underscores count as three non-word graphemes.

### Counting behavior across stable releases

The standalone punctuation checks include two similar-looking marks.

| Character | Name | Unicode | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `٫` | Arabic decimal separator | `U+066B` | `١٫٢` |
| `,` | ASCII comma | `U+002C` | `one, two` |

The table shows how the segments in `they’re 3.14 ___ © ©️ 1️⃣ 世界。` are classified. Both totals exclude spaces.

| Stable releases tested | `they’re` and `3.14` | `___` | `©` / `©️` | `1️⃣` | `世界` | `。` | Words | Total with non-words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| `v0.0.1`–`v0.0.7` | Both words | Word | Both omitted | Word | Word | Omitted | 5 | — |
| `v0.0.8`–`v0.1.6` | Both words | Word | Symbol / emoji | Word | Word | Punctuation | 5 | 8 |
| `v0.1.7`–`v0.1.9` | Both punctuation | Punctuation | Symbol / emoji | Emoji | Word | Punctuation | 1 | 8 |
| **`v0.2.0`** | **Both words** | **Punctuation** | Symbol / emoji | Emoji | Word | Punctuation | **3** | **8** |

An em dash (—) indicates that non-word collection was unavailable. Use `--non-words` to collect punctuation entries.

Results matched across the tested Node.js versions with default locale settings.
